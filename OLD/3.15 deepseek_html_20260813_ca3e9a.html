Goal

Establish cleanliness on public roads and places in India.

भारत में आम सड़क और अन्य जगहों में स्वच्छता प्रस्थापित करें

Stop the litter at the source

Prevent people from generating garbage — empty cigarette packs, paper, spit — on public roads, through direct, emotionally grounded conversations and leaflets, right where littering happens.

Get bins installed and maintained

Appeal to municipal authorities and large corporations to fund, place, and maintain small garbage-collection bins — with an attendant — on public roads.

Strengthen enforcement

Improve the system that prevents households from dumping garbage directly onto roads — including voluntary ambassadors with authority to flag repeat offenders.

🚫 स्रोत पर कचरा रोकें

लोगों को आम सड़क पर इधर-उधर कचरा (जैसे खाली सिगरेट पैकेट, कागज-कपड़े, थूक) फेंकने से रोकें।

🗑️ डिब्बे स्थापित करें

महानगर पालिका प्रशासन या बड़ी कॉरपोरेट कंपनियों को छोटे कचरे के डिब्बे स्थापना व व्यवस्था के लिए आवाहन करें।

⚖️ प्रवर्तन मजबूत करें

अपने घर का कचरा सड़कों पर फेंकने वाले लोगों को प्रतिबंध करने की व्यवस्था दुरुस्त करें।

Benefits

Cleanliness is a key to a healthier, happier world.

Public spaces are where a nation's discipline becomes visible. Three reasons this mission is worth the effort:

Public health

Less waste on roads means fewer breeding grounds for disease and safer streets for daily life.

Environment

Litter-free roads mean less plastic and waste entering drains, soil, and eventually water systems.

Economy & pride

Cleaner public spaces enhance India's global image, improve tourism, and strengthen civic pride.
